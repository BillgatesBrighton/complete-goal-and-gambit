Ranked Match Rules

  • Both players must play the assigned eFootball match.
  • The match room code is provided through the GOAL$GAMBIT Match Hub.
  • Players must submit their result after the match has finished.
  • Results are compared between both players before settlement.
  • One player may be randomly selected for screenshot verification.
  • A disputed result is not automatically paid out.
