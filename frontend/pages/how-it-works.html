1. Create your account

Create your GOAL$GAMBIT player account and set up your competitive profile.

2. Choose your game

Select Chess or Konami eFootball depending on where you want to compete.

3. Find your opponent

Choose an available challenge and prepare to play.

4. Play the match

Chess matches can be played through the GOAL$GAMBIT chess area. eFootball matches are played through Konami eFootball.

5. Record the result

Follow the match-result process provided by the platform after your game.

6. Build your record

Your completed matches can contribute to your player statistics and competitive record.
